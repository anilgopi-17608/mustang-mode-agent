What's Under the Hood

🎯

Skill-Based Matching

Tracks 35+ engineering skills (SolidWorks, CATIA, AutoCAD, NX CAD, orthopedic implants, HVAC) for precise matching.

🧠

AI Reasoning

Gemini AI analyzes each job and explains WHY it fits — not just keyword scores. Includes concerns and red flags.

📍

Location Intelligence

Boosts Hyderabad jobs by +15%, remote jobs by +12%. Knows Indian cities and recognizes "WFH" patterns.

💰

Salary Extraction

Recognizes Indian formats (LPA, CTC, ₹), USD ($), ranges, and CTC variants via regex.

✍️

Cover Letter Openers

For top jobs, AI generates a one-sentence cover letter opener tailored to my background. Copy-paste ready.

Cloud Deployed

Runs on GitHub Actions with encrypted secrets. No server to maintain. No laptop needed. ₹0/month forever.
